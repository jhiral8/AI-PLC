# Research log

Evidence behind the passwordless login idea. Each item is an **EV-** definition that insights cite.

- **EV-1**: Interviews with 6 mobile customers, 8-12 Sep 2026. 4 of 6 said they had abandoned a purchase at the login screen in the last month because they could not remember a password.

- **EV-2**: Support ticket export, Aug 2026. 1,840 of 6,120 tickets (30%) were password resets.

- **EV-3**: Analytics funnel, Aug 2026. 22% of mobile sessions that reach login leave without signing in, against 9% on desktop.
